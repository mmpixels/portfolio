ScopeGuard: A Paper Trail for Scope Changes

On agency projects, scope changes get agreed on calls, in Figma comments and in Slack, and then nobody can find them. ScopeGuard gives every change a record: what was asked, what it costs, and what the client said. I designed it and built it as a working app.
