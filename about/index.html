About me

I'm Manasvi, a UX Designer who's always curious about the why behind things. I like noticing the little details, questioning what's taken for granted, and understanding what makes something work the way it does.

What draws me to design is the opportunity to make sense of things, to take something complicated and find a simpler, more thoughtful way forward. I believe good design comes from understanding what really matters, knowing what to leave out, and making experiences feel natural rather than overcomplicated. For me, it's as much about asking the right questions as it is about finding the answers.

Where I'd like to go next

I'm looking to grow into more product-focused design roles where I can take ownership of meaningful problems, explore ideas, and see them through from concept to execution.

I'd love to work with curious teams that value thoughtful collaboration and design for both the people using and building a product.
